Future Roadmap

The SC-DEX roadmap outlines the major phases and goals for the standard's development and adoption. The aim is to refine the core schema, develop extensions for specific use cases, and facilitate the adoption of the SC-DEX standard by key organizations.

Q4 2024​

  • Schema Finalization: A comprehensive table of accepted location identifiers (e.g., OSID, Varda Agriculture Global Field ID), organization identifiers (e.g., LEI), and relationship types will be added to the SC-DEX documentation. These standardized identifiers will facilitate consistent and interoperable data sharing across supply chains.
  • Public Review: The core schema and initial extensions will undergo a public review phase. Contributions are welcome from all stakeholders, including organizations, data service providers, and civil society partners.
  • Governance Model: Finalize and approve the governance model, including the processes for managing future updates and extensions.
  • Extensions: Begin integrating specific use case extensions, including those for responsible recruiting, living wage tracking, and environmental sustainability.
  • Pilot Programs: Launch pilot programs with key organizations to demonstrate the practical implementation of the SC-DEX standard.

Overview of 2025​

While 2024 is focused on establishing the core schema and foundational aspects of SC-DEX, 2025 will be about scaling the initiative by creating robust extensions and use cases through partnerships and pilot projects. The year will focus on collaborating with various stakeholders to develop tools, integrate the standard into supply chains, and ensure widespread adoption.

2025 Timeline Summary​

  • Q1: Socialization efforts and early pilot development.
  • Q2: Publishing of key resources, pilot launch, and governance setup.
  • Q3: Focus on earned media, tool development, and expanding partnerships.
  • Q4: Completion of pilot projects and planning for AI-driven extensions.

Core Activities​

1. Socialization (General Supply Chain Community)​

This phase involves taking SC-DEX to the broader supply chain community to attract new partners and showcase pilot projects.

  • Storytelling/Sharing: SC-DEX will be presented at conferences like OECD Forum on Due Diligence, Sustainable Brands, and Global Supply Chain Council. Partnerships will also be developed with research institutions such as MIT Supply Chain & Logistics Center and Spatial Finance Institute.
  • Community Activation: Hackathons, workshops, and webinars will be conducted to identify pilots and further engage with local communities and niche supply chain organizations. Events will also target policy implementors to help promote SC-DEX for regulatory compliance.

2. Research & Development (Technical Community)​

  • Standard Maintenance: Ongoing updates to the core schema, and integration of new extensions. Partnerships with data stewards, such as Open Ownership and GS1, will help shape new data attributes in collaboration with civil society organizations.
  • Tool Creation: Projects will include the development of tools such as search and lookup functionalities, data formatting tools, and integrations with data platforms like AWS, Google Cloud, and Open Supply Hub.

Partnerships with research institutions and organizations looking for extensible data standards, such as UNECE and UN Global Compact, will help develop adaptive extensions for compliance, sustainability, and operational efficiency.

3. Implementation Pilots (Organizational Partnerships)​

Pilots will be developed in partnership with various organizations across different types of supply chains. These pilots will demonstrate SC-DEX’s utility across different sectors and complexities:

  • Small Corporation: A pilot with a small corporation that operates in a single sector, with localized supply chains. This is ideal for demonstrating SC-DEX’s ease of adoption in contained, single-tier supply chains.
  • Larger Corporation: A pilot with a corporation with cross-sector or multi-region supply chains, involving multiple tiers of suppliers.
  • Multinational Corporation: A pilot with a large multinational company relying on data service providers for due diligence and audits, showcasing SC-DEX’s ability to standardize complex, multi-tier supply chain data.

Multistakeholder Pilots​

Multistakeholder pilots will involve a combination of corporations, data service providers, and civil society organizations. For example, a pilot may involve a corporation as the data publisher, a data service provider (e.g., audit or compliance platform) as the intermediary, and a civil society organization that provides access to on-the-ground data or stakeholder insights.

These pilots will demonstrate how SC-DEX can be used to streamline data across organizations with different roles in the supply chain ecosystem.

2025 Roadmap Overview​

Q1 2025​

  • Socialization: Participate in supply chain and data standardization webinars and conferences. Develop relationships with key data community leads.
  • R&D: Establish relationships with academic institutions for research and development, and onboard a Technical Manager.
  • Pilots: Develop early pilot partnerships, host a hackathon to generate ideas, and plan initial pilot timelines.

  • R&D: Run a fellowship program for tool development on top of SC-DEX and strengthen partnerships with academic institutions.
  • Pilots: Announce pilot partnerships and begin implementation across multiple sectors, including corporate and civil society collaborations.

Funding and Sustainability​

Fundraising is essential to support the 2025 goals of SC-DEX, including outreach, tool development, and pilot management. We encourage individuals and organizations to get involved in roles that align with their professional goals, KPIs, or organizational metrics:

  • Technical Manager: Oversee SC-DEX’s technical development, leading tool creation and standard maintenance projects.
  • Pilot Project Manager: Lead pilot implementations, manage timelines, and ensure data is exchanged on schedule.
  • Partnerships & Strategy Manager: Develop and maintain relationships with key partners and coordinate pilot opportunities.

These roles provide valuable experience in advancing supply chain transparency and open data initiatives. Reach out to discuss how these opportunities can align with your professional goals and objectives.
